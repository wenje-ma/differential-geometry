# 0923 微分几何: 切线像, 球面曲线与包络

## 8. 切线像: 曲率与挠率

**例 8.1 (切线像)**: 

设 $\boldsymbol\gamma=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(s\right)$ 是以弧长为参数的正则曲线, 且 $\kappa\left(s\right)\neq0$ 对一切 $s$ 成立, 则

$$
\boldsymbol\gamma^{*}\left(s\right):=\boldsymbol T\left(s\right),
$$

也是正则曲线, 且 $\boldsymbol\gamma^{*}\subset S^{2}\left(1\right)$, 称为 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 的**切线像**. 求 $\boldsymbol\gamma^{*}$ 的曲率 $\kappa^{*}$ 与挠率 $\tau^{*}$.

**推导 8.A (例 8.1)**: 

(1) **弧长参数**. 由 $\dot{\boldsymbol\gamma}^{*}=\dot{\boldsymbol T}=\kappa\boldsymbol N$:

$$
\begin{aligned}&&\mathrm ds^{*}&=\left|\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol\gamma^{*}}{\mathrm ds}\right|\mathrm ds=\kappa\left(s\right)\mathrm ds\\
\Rightarrow&&s^{*}&=C_{0}+\int_{0}^{s}\kappa\left(t\right)\mathrm dt.
\end{aligned}
$$

于是 $\boldsymbol T^{*}=\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol\gamma^{*}}{\mathrm ds^{*}}=\frac{\mathrm ds}{\mathrm ds^{*}}\dot{\boldsymbol T}=\frac1\kappa\cdot\kappa\boldsymbol N=\boldsymbol N$.

(2) **曲率** $\kappa^{*}$. 由弗雷内方程 $\dot{\boldsymbol N}=-\kappa\boldsymbol T+\tau\boldsymbol B$:

$$
\begin{aligned}&&\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol T^{*}}{\mathrm ds^{*}}&=\frac1\kappa\dot{\boldsymbol N}=-\boldsymbol T+\frac\tau\kappa\boldsymbol B\\
\Rightarrow&&\kappa^{*}&=\left\|\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol T^{*}}{\mathrm ds^{*}}\right\|=\sqrt{1+\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

(3) **标架**. 主法向与副法向分别为

$$
\begin{aligned}\boldsymbol N^{*}&=\frac{-\boldsymbol T+\frac\tau\kappa\boldsymbol B}{\sqrt{1+\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)^{2}}},\\
\boldsymbol B^{*}&=\boldsymbol T^{*}\wedge\boldsymbol N^{*}
=\frac{\boldsymbol B+\frac\tau\kappa\boldsymbol T}{\sqrt{1+\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)^{2}}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

(4) **挠率** $\tau^{*}$. 由弗雷内方程 $\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol B^{*}}{\mathrm ds^{*}}=-\tau^{*}\boldsymbol N^{*}$. 记 $\lambda:=\frac\tau\kappa$, 由于 $-\tau\boldsymbol N+\lambda\kappa\boldsymbol N=\boldsymbol0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\mathrm d\boldsymbol B^{*}}{\mathrm ds^{*}}
&=\frac1\kappa\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\frac{\boldsymbol B+\lambda\boldsymbol T}{\sqrt{1+\lambda^{2}}}\\
&=\frac1\kappa\left[\frac{-\tau\boldsymbol N+\lambda'\boldsymbol T+\lambda\kappa\boldsymbol N}{\sqrt{1+\lambda^{2}}}
-\frac{\lambda\lambda'\left(\boldsymbol B+\lambda\boldsymbol T\right)}{\left(1+\lambda^{2}\right)^{3/2}}\right]\\
&=\frac1\kappa\left[\frac{\lambda'\boldsymbol T}{\sqrt{1+\lambda^{2}}}
-\frac{\lambda\lambda'\left(\boldsymbol B+\lambda\boldsymbol T\right)}{\left(1+\lambda^{2}\right)^{3/2}}\right]\\
&=\frac1\kappa\cdot\frac{\lambda'\left(\boldsymbol T-\lambda\boldsymbol B\right)}{\left(1+\lambda^{2}\right)^{3/2}}
=-\frac1\kappa\cdot\frac{\lambda'}{1+\lambda^{2}}\boldsymbol N^{*}.
\end{aligned}
$$

其中最后一步用到 $\boldsymbol T-\lambda\boldsymbol B=-\sqrt{1+\lambda^{2}}\boldsymbol N^{*}$. 故

$$
\boxed{\kappa^{*}=\sqrt{1+\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)^{2}},\quad
\tau^{*}=\frac1\kappa\cdot\frac{\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)'}{1+\left(\frac\tau\kappa\right)^{2}}}
$$

## 9. 法平面过定点

**例 9.1 (法平面过定点)**: 

设 $\boldsymbol\gamma=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(s\right)$ 是弧长参数化的正则曲线, 且 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 在每一点的法平面 $\left\{\boldsymbol x:\left\langle\boldsymbol x-\boldsymbol\gamma\left(s\right),\boldsymbol T\left(s\right)\right\rangle=0\right\}$ 都经过同一个固定点. 证明 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 是球面曲线.

**证明 9.A (例 9.1)**: 

平移坐标, 使定点为原点, 则

$$
\left\langle\boldsymbol T,\boldsymbol\gamma\right\rangle\equiv0.
$$

将 $\left\|\gamma\right\|^2$ 对 $s$ 求导得

$$
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left\|\boldsymbol\gamma\right\|^2=2\left\langle\boldsymbol T,\boldsymbol\gamma\right\rangle=0.
$$

因此 $\left\|\gamma\right\|^2$ 为常数, $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 是球面曲线. 

## 10. 球面曲线的曲率—挠率刻画

**命题 10.1 (球面曲线判定)**: 

设 $\boldsymbol\gamma=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(s\right)$ 以弧长为参数, 且 $\kappa\left(s\right)\neq0$, $\tau\left(s\right)\neq0$ 对一切 $s$ 成立. 则 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 是球面曲线, 当且仅当

$$
\left(\frac1\kappa\right)^{2}+\left(\frac1\tau\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\frac1\kappa\right)^{2}\equiv\text{常数}>0.
$$

> **注 (边界情形)**: 该命题「$\Leftarrow$」方向要求 $\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'$ 不恒为零 (证明中需除以它; 孤立零点可由连续性延拓). 若 $\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\equiv0$ (即 $\kappa$ 为常数), 常数条件自动成立, 但曲线未必是球面曲线——$\kappa,\tau$ 均为正常数的圆柱螺线即反例. 讲义习题 15 的完整表述是: 满足该常数条件的曲线, **或者是球面曲线, 或者 $\kappa$ 是常数**; 课堂命题 10.1 默认 $\kappa$ 不恒为常数.

**证明 10.A (命题 10.1)**: 

**($\Rightarrow$)** 设 $\boldsymbol\gamma\subset S^{2}\left(R\right)$, 即 $\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol\gamma\right\rangle\equiv R^{2}$. 逐次求导, 由于 $\left\langle\boldsymbol T,\boldsymbol T\right\rangle+\kappa\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol N\right\rangle=0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}&&\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol T\right\rangle&\equiv0\\
&&\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol N\right\rangle&=-\frac1\kappa\\
&&\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,-\kappa\boldsymbol T+\tau\boldsymbol B\right\rangle&=-\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\\
\Rightarrow&&\tau\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol B\right\rangle&=-\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'.
\end{aligned}
$$

即

$$
\begin{aligned}\boldsymbol\gamma&=-\frac1\kappa\boldsymbol N-\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\boldsymbol B\\
\left\|\boldsymbol\gamma\right\|^{2}&=\left(\frac1\kappa\right)^{2}+\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)^{2}\equiv R^{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**($\Leftarrow$)** 令

$$
\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}:=\boldsymbol\gamma+\frac1\kappa\boldsymbol N+\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\boldsymbol B.
$$

由于 $\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'=-\frac{\kappa'}{\kappa^{2}}$, 计算:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\kappa\boldsymbol N\right)
&=-\frac{\kappa'}{\kappa^{2}}\boldsymbol N+\frac1\kappa\left(-\kappa\boldsymbol T+\tau\boldsymbol B\right)\\
&=-\frac{\kappa'}{\kappa^{2}}\boldsymbol N-\boldsymbol T+\frac\tau\kappa\boldsymbol B,\\
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\boldsymbol B\right)&=\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)\boldsymbol B+\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\left(-\tau\boldsymbol N\right)\\
&=\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)\boldsymbol B-\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\boldsymbol N.
\end{aligned}
$$

相加并加上 $\boldsymbol T$ 后, $\boldsymbol N$ 方向系数为 $-\frac{\kappa'}{\kappa^{2}}-\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'=0$, 故

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}}{\mathrm ds}=\left[\frac\tau\kappa+\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)\right]\boldsymbol B.
$$

由常数条件对 $s$ 求导:

$$
2\frac1\kappa\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'+2\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\cdot\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)=0.
$$

在 $\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\neq0$ 处 (否则见命题后的注),

$$
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)=-\frac\tau\kappa.
$$

于是 $\frac{\mathrm d\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}}{\mathrm ds}=\boldsymbol0$, $\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}$ 为常向量; 而

$$
\begin{aligned}\left\|\boldsymbol\gamma-\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}\right\|^{2}&=\left\|\frac1\kappa\boldsymbol N+\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\boldsymbol B\right\|^{2}\\
&=\left(\frac1\kappa\right)^{2}+\left(\frac1\tau\left(\frac1\kappa\right)'\right)^{2}\equiv\text{常数}.
\end{aligned}
$$

故 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 落在以 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol\gamma}$ 为球心的球面上. 

## 11. 球面上的闭曲线: $\displaystyle\int_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\frac\tau\kappa\mathrm ds=0$

**例 11.1 (闭球面曲线上的积分)**: 

设 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 是球面上的闭曲线 ($\kappa,\tau$ 处处非零), 则

$$
\int_{\boldsymbol\gamma}\frac\tau\kappa\mathrm ds=0.
$$

**证明 11.A (例 11.1)**: 

由命题 10.1 的分解, $\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol N\right\rangle=-\frac1\kappa$. 对 $\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol B\right\rangle$ 求导:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol B\right\rangle&=\left\langle\boldsymbol T,\boldsymbol B\right\rangle+\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\dot{\boldsymbol B}\right\rangle\\
&=\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,-\tau\boldsymbol N\right\rangle
=-\tau\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol N\right\rangle=\frac\tau\kappa.
\end{aligned}
$$

因 $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 是闭曲线, 则 $\boldsymbol\gamma\left(L\right)=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(0\right)$, $\boldsymbol B\left(L\right)=\boldsymbol B\left(0\right)$,

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{L}\frac\tau\kappa\mathrm ds&=\int_{0}^{L}\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds}\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol B\right\rangle\mathrm ds\\
&=\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma\left(L\right),\boldsymbol B\left(L\right)\right\rangle-\left\langle\boldsymbol\gamma\left(0\right),\boldsymbol B\left(0\right)\right\rangle=0
\end{aligned}
$$

## 12. 平面曲线族的包络

**定义 12.1 (包络)**: 

设 $\boldsymbol\gamma:J_{t}\times I_{\alpha}\to\mathbb R^{2}$ 是 $C^{k}$ 映射 ($k\ge1$), 记

$$
\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\left(t\right):=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(t;\alpha\right),\quad\alpha\in I,
$$

得一族平面曲线 $\left\{\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\right\}_{\alpha\in I}$. 若存在曲线 $\Gamma$, 使得 $\Gamma$ 与每个 $\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}$ 都相切于某点 $\boldsymbol\gamma\left(t\left(\alpha\right);\alpha\right)$, 则称 $\Gamma$ 是曲线族 $\left\{\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\right\}$ 的一条**包络曲线**.

**命题 12.2 (包络的参数方程)**: 

若 $\Gamma$ 是包络, 则

$$
\Gamma\left(\alpha\right)=\boldsymbol\gamma\left(t\left(\alpha\right);\alpha\right),\quad\alpha\in I,
$$

给出 $\Gamma$ 的参数表示, 其中 $t=t\left(\alpha\right)$ 满足

$$
\det\left.\left(\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial t},\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial\alpha}\right)\middle|\right._{t=t\left(\alpha\right)}=0.
$$

即

$$
\left.\left(\frac{\partial\gamma^{1}}{\partial t}\frac{\partial\gamma^{2}}{\partial\alpha}
-\frac{\partial\gamma^{1}}{\partial\alpha}\frac{\partial\gamma^{2}}{\partial t}\right)\middle|\right._{t=t\left(\alpha\right)}=0
\tag{$*$}.
$$

**推导 12.A (命题 12.2)**: 

由链式法则

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\Gamma}{\mathrm d\alpha}=\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial t}t'\left(\alpha\right)+\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial\alpha}.
$$

相切条件给出 $\frac{\mathrm d\Gamma}{\mathrm d\alpha}$ 与 $\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial t}|_{t=t\left(\alpha\right)}$ 平行, 故二者线性相关, 行列式为零, 即 $\left(*\right)$. 

**命题 12.3 (脊线)**: 

$\left(*\right)$ 的解或者是包络曲线, 或者是曲线族 $\left\{\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\right\}$ 的起点组成的曲线 (**脊线**).

## 13. 无滑动滚动圆上的曲线族的包络

**例 13.1 (滚动圆/齿轮—齿条)**: 

在 $Ox^{1}x^{2}$ 坐标系中, 圆 $\left(x^{1}\right)^{2}+\left(x^{2}-R\right)^{2}=R^{2}$ 沿 $Ox^{1}$ 轴逆时针**无滑动滚动**. 以圆心 $C$ 为原点, 坐标轴与 $Ox^{1},Ox^{2}$ 平行的坐标系 $C\overline x^{1}\overline x^{2}$ 中, 给定附在圆上的曲线

$$
\boldsymbol\gamma:\overline x^{i}=\overline x^{i}\left(\theta\right),i=1,2,
$$

$\theta$ 为圆上的角参数. $\boldsymbol\gamma$ 随圆滚动产生一族曲线 $\left\{\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\right\}$ ($\alpha$ 为滚动角). 求 $\left\{\boldsymbol\gamma_{\alpha}\right\}$ 的包络.

**推导 13.A (例 13.1)**: 

(1) **参数化**. 滚动角 $\alpha$ 后, 曲线先随圆转动 $\alpha$:

$$
\begin{aligned}\overline x^{1}\left(\theta;\alpha\right)&=\overline x^{1}\left(\theta\right)\cos\alpha-\overline x^{2}\left(\theta\right)\sin\alpha,\\
\overline x^{2}\left(\theta;\alpha\right)&=\overline x^{1}\left(\theta\right)\sin\alpha+\overline x^{2}\left(\theta\right)\cos\alpha.
\end{aligned}
$$

再叠加上滚动引起的平移 (无滑动滚动, 圆心前进距离为 $R\alpha$):

$$
\begin{aligned}x^{1}\left(\theta;\alpha\right)&=\overline x^{1}\left(\theta\right)\cos\alpha-\overline x^{2}\left(\theta\right)\sin\alpha+R\alpha,\\
x^{2}\left(\theta;\alpha\right)&=\overline x^{1}\left(\theta\right)\sin\alpha+\overline x^{2}\left(\theta\right)\cos\alpha+R.
\end{aligned}
$$

(2) **代入 $\left(*\right)$**. 先算偏导数:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial x^{1}}{\partial\theta}&=\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\cos\alpha-\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\sin\alpha,\\
\frac{\partial x^{2}}{\partial\alpha}&=\overline x^{1}\cos\alpha-\overline x^{2}\sin\alpha,\\
\frac{\partial x^{1}}{\partial\alpha}&=-\overline x^{1}\sin\alpha-\overline x^{2}\cos\alpha+R,\\
\frac{\partial x^{2}}{\partial\theta}&=\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\sin\alpha+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\cos\alpha.
\end{aligned}
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}\det\left(\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial\theta},\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\gamma}{\partial\alpha}\right)&=\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\cos\alpha-\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\sin\alpha\right]\left[\overline x^{1}\cos\alpha-\overline x^{2}\sin\alpha\right]\\
&\quad\;-\left[-\overline x^{1}\sin\alpha-\overline x^{2}\cos\alpha+R\right]\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\sin\alpha+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\cos\alpha\right]=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

展开并整理得

$$
\left(\overline x^{1}\right)\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'
=R\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\sin\alpha+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\cos\alpha\right].
$$

(3) **解出 $\alpha$**. 令

$$
L\left(\theta\right):=\sqrt{\left(\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\right)^{2}+\left(\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\right)^{2}},
$$

并取相位角 $\psi\left(\theta\right):=\arctan\frac{\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'}{\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'}$, 则

$$
\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'\sin\alpha+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'\cos\alpha=L\sin\left(\alpha+\psi\right).
$$

且左端

$$
\begin{aligned}\left(\overline x^{1}\right)\left(\overline x^{1}\right)'+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)\left(\overline x^{2}\right)'=\frac12\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm d\theta}\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)^{2}+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)^{2}\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}\sin\left(\alpha+\psi\right)&=\frac{\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)^{2}+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)^{2}\right]'}{2RL},\\
\alpha\left(\theta\right)&=\arcsin\frac{\left[\left(\overline x^{1}\right)^{2}+\left(\overline x^{2}\right)^{2}\right]'}{2RL}-\psi\left(\theta\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\Gamma\left(\alpha\left(\theta\right)\right)=\left(x^{1}\left(\theta;\alpha\left(\theta\right)\right),x^{2}\left(\theta;\alpha\left(\theta\right)\right)\right)
$$
